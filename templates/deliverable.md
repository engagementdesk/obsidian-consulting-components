# Deliverable title

> There is no `done` state here, on purpose:
> `not_started` → `in_progress` → `submitted` → `accepted` (or `rejected`)
>
> A deliverable you have finished and the client has not accepted is not revenue. It is risk sitting on
> your side of the table.

## What it is

> The artifact. "A 12-page options paper", not "pricing work".

## Acceptance criteria

> Write this before starting. If you cannot say what "accepted" means, the deliverable is not scoped,
> and you are about to do work whose completion is someone else's opinion.
>
> Good criteria are checkable by a third party. "Covers all four product lines with a recommendation
> and a sensitivity range for each" is a criterion. "Is useful" is not.
